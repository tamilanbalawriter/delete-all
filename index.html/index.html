Kavithai Padal Kathai Jokes

📖 Kavithai - Mazhai

Mazhai vandhal manam kulirum,
Mann vasam manadhai thodum,
Ninaivugal ellam malarum,
En kavithaiyil uyir perum.

- Tamilanbala

📖 Kavithai - Amma

Amma endra sol thaan,
Ulagathin miga periya kavithai,
Anbu endral amma thaan,
Aayul muluvathum thunai aval thaan.

🎵 Padal - Nenjam

Nenjamellam un ninaivu,
Kangalellam un kanavu,
Vazhkkai payanam neeyae thunai,
En paadalukku neeyae isai.

📚 Siru Kathai - Chinna Payanam

Oru chinna gramathil oru payyan irundhan. Avanukku periya kanavu irundhadhu - ezhuthalar aaganum endru. Thinamum oru pakkam ezhudhinaan. Oru naal avan ezhuthu palarai sirikka vaithadhu, sila perai azha vaithadhu. Adhu thaan avan vetri!

Needhi: Thinamum konjam ezhudhunga, periya maattram varum.
